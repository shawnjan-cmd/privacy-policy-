3. ANDROID PERMISSIONS

📷
CAMERA
Used exclusively to scan QR codes for pairing your phone with your PC. No images are captured, stored, or transmitted. Camera is opened only when you tap "Scan QR Code" and closes immediately after scanning. Foreground use only.
🌐
INTERNET
Required to communicate with your PC server over your local Wi-Fi. All network requests go to your own PC's local IP address only. No external internet connections are made.
📶
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE / ACCESS_WIFI_STATE
Used to detect if you are connected to Wi-Fi before attempting to connect to your PC. Network state is checked locally. No data is transmitted.
🖼️
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
Used for the File Share feature to allow you to select images to transfer to your PC. Selected files are sent directly to your PC over your local network. Nothing is uploaded to any cloud or external server.

PERMISSIONS WE DO NOT REQUEST:

Location (GPS) · Microphone · Contacts · Phone state · SMS · Bluetooth · Body sensors · Activity recognition · Advertising ID (blocked in manifest)

4. LOCAL AI FEATURES

LOCAL OLLAMA AI (Butler AI Chat)

The Butler AI Chat connects to an Ollama AI model running on YOUR OWN PC. This is a locally-hosted large language model (LLM).

  • No data is sent to OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, or any AI cloud service
  • All AI conversations stay within your local network
  • Conversation history may be stored locally on your device for context
  • The AI model runs entirely on your own PC hardware

KNOWLEDGE BASE (On-Device)

  • Generated locally from your script activity
  • Stored only on your device
  • Never transmitted externally
  • Fully deletable from Settings → Knowledge Base → Clear

6. DATA DELETION

All data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time:

  1. In-App: Settings → Data & Storage → Clear App Cache
  2. Android: Settings → Apps → Butler AI → Storage → Clear Data
  3. Knowledge Base only: App Settings → Knowledge Base → Clear
  4. Uninstall: Removes all data permanently

For a full guide to deleting your data, see the Delete My Data page.
