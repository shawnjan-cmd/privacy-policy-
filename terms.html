1. WHAT BUTLER AI DOES

Butler AI connects your Android device to a Python server (butler_server.py) running on your own personal computer over your local Wi-Fi network. You can send Python scripts to your PC, chat with a local AI model (Ollama), monitor system resources, and transfer files — all entirely within your own private network.

Butler AI is built for power users and developers who want to automate tasks on their own computers. It is NOT intended for unauthorized remote access to any system.
