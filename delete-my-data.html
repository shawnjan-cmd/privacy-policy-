YOUR DATA IS ALREADY YOURS

Butler AI stores all data locally on your device. There are no cloud servers, no accounts, and no remote databases to clear. Deleting your data is instant and permanent.

3 TAPS
Settings → DELETE ALL MY DATA → Confirm

METHOD 1 — IN-APP (RECOMMENDED)

★
Delete from within Butler AI
FASTEST

The quickest way to delete everything — all scripts, KB entries, chat history, and settings in one tap.

  • Open the Butler AI app on your phone
  • Tap the Settings tab (gear icon, bottom nav)
  • Scroll down to "Data & Storage" section
  • Tap "DELETE ALL MY DATA"
  • Confirm in the dialog — deletion is immediate and permanent

⚡ This removes: script library, execution history, Knowledge Base, chat history, connection settings, pairing tokens, and all preferences.

METHOD 2 — ANDROID SYSTEM SETTINGS

2
Clear via Android App Settings

Use this if you can't open the app:

  • Open Android Settings
  • Go to Apps (or Application Manager)
  • Find and tap Butler AI: PC Automation
  • Tap Storage
  • Tap Clear Data — this removes all app data instantly

METHOD 3 — UNINSTALL THE APP

3
Uninstall Butler AI

Uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored data. Android automatically clears all AsyncStorage, cached files, and settings when an app is uninstalled.

  • Long-press the Butler AI icon on your home screen
  • Select Uninstall
  • Confirm — all data is deleted permanently

4
